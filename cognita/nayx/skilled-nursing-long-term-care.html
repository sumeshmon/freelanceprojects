Nayx is a dual marketplace technology platform that connects skilled nursing facilities (SNFs), long-term care centers, and post-acute rehabilitation facilities directly with verified independent clinical professionals. By replacing slow, costly staffing brokers with automated real-time routing, Nayx empowers post-acute care administrators to cover open per-diem shifts in minutes while replacing bloated agency margins with transparent, predictable pricing.

Post-acute facilities face intense pressure to meet state-mandated direct care nursing hours per patient day (HPPD) while managing strict operating budgets. Traditional staffing agencies charge 40% to 60% hidden markups and rely on manual recruiter chains that leave shifts unfilled, risking operational citations and care disruptions. Nayx delivers a direct, transparent alternative—matching qualified independent per-diem RNs, LPNs/LVNs, and CNAs to open shifts on demand with absolute rate transparency for both facilities and clinicians.

How Nayx Works for
Skilled Nursing Facilities

01

Post Shift Demand

Facility Directors of Nursing (DON) post per-diem or recurring shifts directly through the central dashboard, setting required certifications (RN, LPN, CNA), shift times, and baseline hourly rates.

02

Autonomous AI Matching

Our platform evaluates clinician credentials, proximity, and experience in long-term care environments to route open requisitions to matched independent clinical professionals in minutes.

03

One-Click Shift Acceptance

Pre-verified clinicians accept shifts with a single tap. Automated digital onboarding ensures complete credential alignment before arrival on site.

04

On-Device Documentation & Shift Verification

Clinicians utilize on-device tools to complete shift check-ins and summary capture. Our point-of-care capture engine is built to support HIPAA compliance by processing encounter notes locally on the provider's hardware, backed by executed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).

05

Unified Dashboard Analytics

Facility leaders track fill rates, monitor HPPD metrics, verify digital timecards, and streamline billing from one centralized workforce automation platform.
