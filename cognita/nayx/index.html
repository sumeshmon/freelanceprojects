How Nayx Works

A simple 4-step process connecting top clinical talent with healthcare facilities through autonomous AI agents.

1. Post Shift Demand

Clinicians set career preferences via AI. Facilities post open shifts, pay rates, and requirements directly on their dashboard.

2. Autonomous AI Matching

Intelligent algorithms match credentials, specialty, location, and availability to pair open shifts with qualified talent instantly.

3. One-Click Placement

Automated credentialing and digital onboarding enable one-click shift acceptance—eliminating recruiter delays.

4. Transparent Approval & Fast Payouts

Verified work logs trigger fast clinician payouts and real-time workforce analytics for facilities.

Nayx is an AI-powered healthcare workforce and practice management platform that connects hospitals, clinics, and health systems directly with verified clinical talent. By replacing traditional middleman agencies with intelligent automation, Nayx eliminates hidden markups, offers 100% rate transparency, and provides clinicians with built-in, on-device AI documentation.

The AI career agent serves as an autonomous job-matching partner. Clinicians simply set their location boundaries, specialty, availability, and target pay rates. The automated AI agent continuously scans, filters, and applies to matching shifts on autopilot, removing the hassle of manual job hunting.

Nayx operates a dual marketplace serving both Healthcare Professionals (nurses, physicians, and allied health workers looking for flexible shifts and career control) and Healthcare Facilities (hospitals, private clinics, telehealth providers, and rural care centers seeking fast, transparent staffing).

Traditional agencies rely on slow recruiter phone calls and charge 40%–60% markups. Nayx uses real-time algorithmic matching to connect pre-verified, nearby clinicians with vacant shifts in sub-10 milliseconds. Facilities get transparent, upfront pricing with 0% hidden agency fees, lowering labor costs while ensuring clinicians earn competitive rates.

Before clinicians are matched, Nayx performs automated credentialing, state license verification, and background checks. Facility administrators can review audit-ready, verified credentials, work history, and peer ratings directly on their central dashboard before confirming a booking.

Nayx includes a native, offline AI documentation feature that allows clinicians to generate structured SOAP notes from patient encounters in seconds. Because all processing happens 100% locally on the clinician's physical device, no audio or note data is sent to the cloud. This guarantees 100% offline functionality (in signal-dead zones) while maintaining strict HIPAA and data privacy compliance.
