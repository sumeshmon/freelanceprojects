How Nayx Works

A simple 4-step process connecting top clinical talent with healthcare facilities through autonomous AI agents.

1. Post Shift Demand

Clinicians set career preferences via AI. Facilities post open shifts, pay rates, and requirements directly on their dashboard.

2. Autonomous AI Matching

Intelligent algorithms match credentials, specialty, location, and availability to pair open shifts with qualified talent instantly.

3. One-Click Placement

Automated credentialing and digital onboarding enable one-click shift acceptance—eliminating recruiter delays.

4. Transparent Approval & Fast Payouts

Verified work logs trigger fast clinician payouts and real-time workforce analytics for facilities.
