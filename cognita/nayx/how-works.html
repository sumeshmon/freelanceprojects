The 4-Step
Autonomous Workflow

Post Shift Demand & Set Preferences

For Facilities: Post shift criteria, clinical specialty requirements, unit needs, and pay rates directly through the centralized web app.

For Clinicians: Input target compensation, preferred shifts, travel boundaries, and clinical specialties in the mobile app.

Autonomous AI Algorithmic Matching

Nayx intelligent algorithms instantly analyze credentials, medical specialties, geolocation proximity, and live schedule availability.

Open shift postings match with pre-verified clinicians in sub-10 milliseconds—eliminating slow middleman agency phone chains completely.

One-Click Placement & Automated Verification

Clinicians receive tailored shift notifications and accept placements with a single tap on their mobile device.

The platform automatically verifies state licenses, background checks, and certifications, delivering an audit-ready compliance profile to facility leaders.

Transparent Approval & Fast Direct Payouts

Verified digital work logs and timecards are submitted instantly upon shift completion for supervisor approval.

Approved shifts trigger fast electronic direct deposits to clinicians, while facility leaders receive real-time workforce performance analytics and labor expense logs.
