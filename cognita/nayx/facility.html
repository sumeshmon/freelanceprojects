Nayx changes that.

We built Nayx to give facility administrators, nurse managers, and clinical leaders complete control over their workforce operations. By replacing middleman brokers with real-time autonomous AI matching, Nayx connects your hospital, clinic, or health system directly with pre-screened, credentialed clinicians when you need them most.

Whether you need to cover last-minute sick calls, absorb unexpected volume spikes, or secure ongoing locum coverage, Nayx delivers an automated, transparent, and care-first staffing solution.
