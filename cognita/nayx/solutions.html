Modern healthcare delivery demands speed, flexibility, and absolute precision. Legacy staffing models trap health systems in bloated overhead costs and leave clinicians burnt out under administrative burdens.

Nayx brings Autonomous Workforce Engineering to the entire healthcare continuum. By combining sub-10ms real-time shift execution, automated primary-source compliance, and on-device HIPAA-compliant AI charting, Nayx transforms how healthcare talent is deployed, managed, and compensated.

Core Platform Solutions
We Offer

Autonomous Shift Dispatch Engine

Replaces manual recruiter chains with sub-10ms algorithmic matching. Matches shift criteria directly with pre-screened clinicians based on skills, location, and rate preferences. Features sub-10ms matching algorithms, real-time geolocation dispatch, and priority backup auto-relisting.

Automated Credentialing & Vault

A centralized digital vault that automatically monitors state licenses, certifications, and background checks against primary sources before every booking. Features primary source verification, audit-ready compliance records, and digital credential storage.

On-Device Offline AI Documentation

A privacy-first charting assistant that listens to patient encounters offline and generates structured SOAP notes locally without sending audio to cloud servers. Features 100% on-device processing, 40+ specialized medical templates, and direct EHR export capabilities.

Enterprise Float Pool & VMS Middleware

A centralized management web app allowing facilities to coordinate internal float pools alongside external contingent talent from one interface. Features enterprise VMS/EHR API integration, labor analytics, expense logs, and automated direct payouts.
