Modern healthcare delivery demands speed, flexibility, and absolute precision. Legacy staffing models trap health systems in bloated overhead costs and leave clinicians burnt out under administrative burdens.

Nayx brings workforce automation to the entire healthcare continuum. By combining automated real-time shift routing, primary-source compliance in alignment with Joint Commission credentialing standards, and on-device HIPAA-aligned AI charting, Nayx transforms how healthcare talent is deployed, managed, and compensated.

Core Platform Solutions
We Offer

Autonomous Shift Dispatch Engine

Replaces manual recruiter chains with automated real-time routing. Matches shift criteria directly with pre-screened independent clinical professionals based on skills, location, and rate preferences to maintain state minimum nurse-to-patient staffing ratios. Features rapid minutes-to-fill routing, real-time geolocation dispatch, and priority backup auto-relisting.

Automated Credentialing & Vault

A centralized digital vault that automatically monitors state licenses, certifications, and background checks against primary sources before every booking. Features primary source verification in alignment with Joint Commission credentialing standards, audit-ready compliance records, and digital credential storage for seamless CMS alignment.

On-Device Offline AI Documentation (Nayx Scribe AI)

A privacy-first charting assistant that listens to patient encounters offline and generates structured SOAP notes locally without sending audio to cloud servers— built to support HIPAA compliance backed by executed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). Features 100% on-device processing, 40+ specialized medical templates, and direct EHR export capabilities.

Enterprise Float Pool & VMS Middleware

A centralized management web app allowing facilities to coordinate internal float pools alongside locum tenens connections from one interface. Features enterprise VMS/EHR API integration, labor analytics, expense logs, and automated direct payouts on our dual marketplace technology platform.
