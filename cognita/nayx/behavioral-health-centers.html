De-Escalating the
Psychiatric Workforce Crisis

Operating a specialized behavioral health center, acute psychiatric unit, or addiction treatment facility requires uninterrupted coverage by highly trained, crisis-literate clinicians. Traditional placement brokers struggle with high-acuity credentialing, subjecting facility leadership to 24–72 hour delays and 40% to 60% third-party markups—all while leaving specialized units understaffed.

Nayx replaces manual recruiters with an automated dual marketplace technology platform. By pairing automated real-time routing with strict primary-source credential verification, Nayx connects behavioral health administrators directly to pre-screened independent clinical professionals specializing in psychiatric care.
