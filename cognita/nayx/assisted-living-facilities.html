The Modern Alternative to
Legacy Senior Care Staffing Brokers

Managing an assisted living facility or memory care community requires maintaining precise caregiver-to-resident ratios without ballooning labor budgets. Traditional staffing brokers burden senior care operators with 40% to 60% markups and slow manual recruiting chains.

Nayx changes this by operating as a direct dual marketplace technology platform. Instead of relying on middlemen, our software uses automated real-time routing to connect resident care directors directly with pre-screened independent clinical professionals—filling critical open per-diem shifts in minutes with complete rate transparency.

How Nayx Works for
Assisted Living Communities

01

Post Shift Requirements

Assisted living administrators or resident care directors post open per-diem or recurring shifts directly on the central management dashboard—defining required role certifications (RN, LPN, Med Tech, Caregiver), shift hours, and baseline pay rates.

02

Autonomous AI Matching

Our platform evaluates clinician qualifications, proximity, and experience in senior living and memory care settings to route open requisitions to matched independent clinical professionals in minutes.

03

One-Click Shift Acceptance

Pre-verified professionals review shift details and accept requisitions instantly. Automated digital onboarding ensures complete credential alignment before arriving at your senior living community.

04

On-Device Point-of-Care Capture & Check-In

Clinicians log shift check-ins and complete point-of-care summaries using native mobile software. Our documentation engine is built to support HIPAA compliance by processing encounter summaries locally on the physical device, backed by executed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).

05

Unified Facility Dashboard

Community directors track fill rates, review digital timecards, monitor shift expenditure, and streamline operations from one centralized workforce automation platform.
