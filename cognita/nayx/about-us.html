Our Purpose

Reimagining Healthcare
Workforce Connectivity

Traditional healthcare staffing relies on opaque recruiter networks, lengthy credential verification cycles, and excessive markups ranging from 40% to 60%. Nayx replaces these manual workflows with an algorithmic dual-marketplace ecosystem.

We built Nayx around a fundamental truth: When clinicians are respected, supported, and given autonomy, patient care thrives.

Our platform introduces an Autonomous AI Career Agent that works as a personal 24/7 advocate for healthcare workers. Clinicians simply set their desired schedules, pay requirements, travel boundaries, and clinical preferences. Our intelligent algorithms handle the rest on autopilot—matching, filtering, and securing ideal shifts without the hassle of recruiter phone calls or aggressive sales tactics.

For healthcare organizations, Nayx transforms staffing from a constant crisis into an automated, predictable workflow. Facility leaders can instantly post shift demands, verify audit-ready credentials, streamline onboarding, and monitor workforce analytics through a single central dashboard—all with zero hidden agency fees.
