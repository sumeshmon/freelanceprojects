Redefining
Clinical Documentation Security

Nayx Scribe AI is an intelligent medical documentation tool built directly into the Nayx mobile app to save clinicians hours of manual paperwork.

Healthcare facilities rely on contingent clinicians to maintain care ratios, but traditional cloud-based AI scribes introduce severe privacy friction. Streaming live patient audio over the internet to remote servers exposes health systems to data breaches, unauthorized data scraping, and HIPAA violations—especially when staff use temporary or personal devices.

Nayx Scribe AI eliminates this liability by processing 100% of audio parsing, voice-to-text conversion, and SOAP note generation locally on the provider's physical device hardware. Patient Health Information (PHI) never leaves the device, eliminating cloud exposure while providing automation documentation capabilities at the point of care—ensuring complete HIPAA compliance, executed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) alignment, and secure EHR synchronization followed by an immediate local memory purge.

Core Operational Advantages of
Nayx Scribe AI

Zero-Cloud Local Neural Processing

Nayx Scribe AI runs optimized edge models directly on the mobile device's Neural Processing Unit (NPU). Voice data is parsed entirely in temporary memory (RAM) and destroyed the moment documentation is finalized—guaranteeing zero server transmission or external storage.

Automated Multi-Specialty SOAP Formatting

Transform unstructured room conversations into precise, audit-ready clinical notes in seconds:

  • Subjective: Automates chief complaints, historical data, and patient-reported symptoms.
  • Objective: Organizes physical exam observations, vital trends, and diagnostic inputs.
  • Assessment: Synthesizes clinical reasoning and differential considerations.
  • Plan: Structurally outputs treatment orders, prescriptions, and follow-up directives.
Real-Time EHR Ecosystem Integration

Once verified by the clinician, completed documentation pushes directly into your enterprise system via secure FHIR/HL7 API webhooks. The system verifies receipt, purges local cache strings, and logs the entry straight into patient records.

Complete Legal & Regulatory Safeguards

Designed from the ground up to support HIPAA compliance, Nayx Scribe AI operates under executed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with every facility partner, ensuring total regulatory alignment across all care environments.
