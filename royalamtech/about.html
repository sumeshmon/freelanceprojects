About Us

Welcome to Royal Amtech, a distinguished business establishment with a decade of excellence in Kottiyam, Kerala. Founded on principles of transparency and efficiency, our organization thrives on a commitment to fostering genuine talent without the need for intermediaries or agencies.

Established in 2013, Royal Amtech has grown into a reputable entity, holding ISO and FSSAI certifications. Our firm commitment to authenticity and straightforward enlistment processes has made us a preferred choice for individuals seeking rapid career advancement.

Our Mission

At Royal Amtech, our mission revolves around providing the most trusted services in the online distribution and marketing field. Over the past years, we have come remarkably close to achieving our mission, setting benchmarks in reliability and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

At Royal Amtech, we envision a transformative role in the vibrant business landscape of Kerala, spearheading a series of groundbreaking ventures that redefine the way we approach commerce. Our vision encompasses the first three strategic steps, focusing on online dissemination and promotion, to usher in a new era of business excellence and customer engagement.
